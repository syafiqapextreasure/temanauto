# TemanAuto search strategy

Research checked on **6 November 2026**, for a Malaysian Malay-language car-broker service. This is an intent-led editorial plan, not a measured keyword ranking. No Keyword Planner account, Search Console property, paid keyword database, or verified monthly-volume export was available. Search visibility, leads, and AI citations are not guaranteed.

## Positioning

TemanAuto's useful distinction is a clear, personal process for people confused by a rejected application, credit records, or inconsistent income. Make the broker role, actual contact details, service area, fees and boundaries explicit. The content should answer the next practical question instead of repeating “mudah lulus.” The approved fictional testimonials must remain labelled until replaced with genuine, consented stories; do not create review or aggregate-rating structured data from them.

## SEO, answer-first content and AI discovery

Use a short direct answer, descriptive headings, actual HTML text, sources and related links. These make pages easier for people and search systems to understand; they do not guarantee an answer box or citation. Google states that its normal SEO practices also apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode, and that no special AI markup or machine-readable file is required. A page needs to be indexed and eligible for a Search snippet. [Google AI features documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

Google's current documentation records that FAQ rich results stopped appearing on 7 May 2026. Keep visible FAQs for readers; do not sell FAQ schema as a route to Google rich results. The same update page says `llms.txt` is not needed for Google Search and does not improve its rankings. [Google documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates)

Financial content needs careful trust signals. Show the real author/business role, date information accurately, provide authoritative references, explain assumptions, and add original experience only when it is genuine. Avoid stock claims of expertise or invented reviewer credentials. [Google people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)

## Implementation requirements

- Render complete HTML on every article URL; do not ship only screenshot images or require a search engine to operate a client-only search form.
- Give each page a unique title, description, canonical URL and share image. Build absolute canonical URLs from the confirmed deployment domain, not a guessed domain.
- Use one main heading per page, ordered section headings, descriptive internal links, breadcrumbs and image alt text. Decorative images should not repeat nearby text for screen readers.
- Keep clean paths, consistent slash policy and redirects for obsolete `.html` URLs. Return an actual 404 for missing pages.
- Include crawlable article links and a sitemap. Do not create thin near-duplicate pages for every salary or place name.
- Include accurate `Organization`, `WebSite`, `BreadcrumbList` and article metadata where relevant. Omit unconfirmed address, phone, opening hours, stock inventory, ratings, pricing and reviewer credentials.
- Keep answer text consistent between visible content and any structured data. Do not imply that machine-readable markup validates financial advice.
- Reserve image dimensions, lazy-load below-the-fold imagery, load the hero promptly, and use local font files with sensible fallbacks. Maintain large readable text, good contrast and usable tap targets.
- Reduce motion when requested by the visitor. Animation should not obscure answers, block navigation or delay the first meaningful content.
- Verify the final domain in Search Console after deployment. Monitor indexing, search terms, qualified enquiries and the pages that help users reach a useful next step.

## Content boundaries

The seven supplied Malay articles distinguish general preparation from loan eligibility. They contain no personalised approval decision, no unverified bank rate, and no promise to remove valid credit history. Each links to official sources relevant to its specific assertions. Questions, checklists and illustrative budget arithmetic are original explanatory material.

For future calculators, clearly state the interest method, rate assumption, term and excluded costs. The [current Maybank hire-purchase page](https://www.maybank2u.com.my/maybank2u/malaysia/en/personal/loans/hire_purchase/hire_purchase.page) describes a reducing-balance framework effective 1 August 2026. Do not reuse an old flat-rate formula as if it described every current financing offer. This observation is product-specific; obtain current lender terms before quoting an offer.

## First review cycle

Launch with accurate contact information and a small useful article set. Check crawlability and metadata once deployed. Then review actual questions and Search Console observations before choosing more topics. Improve the answers that people use; do not create a daily mass-content quota. Recheck lender references when products or requirements change. There is no promised page-one deadline or guaranteed “fast growth” from this work.
